App Builder: privacy

App Builder is a free tool for building app prototypes at school, at tools.learningwaypoints.com/app-builder. It has no server and no accounts of its own.

What stays in your browser

The app you are building, what it keeps between visits, pictures and recordings you add, and your place in a guided tutorial are kept in your own browser on your own computer. They are not sent to App Builder or anyone else.

Signing in with Microsoft or Google

If you choose to sign in with Microsoft or Google, it is only so App Builder can save your apps to, and open them from, your own OneDrive or Google Drive:

  • App Builder only uses an "App Builder" folder in your drive (with Google, only files App Builder made itself).
  • It reads your name and email address only to show who is signed in.
  • The permission is kept in your browser for that tab, and is forgotten when you close the tab or choose Sign out. App Builder does not keep it anywhere else.
  • Your files stay in your own drive, under your school's (or your own) Microsoft or Google account and its rules.

App Builder does not collect, sell or share any information about you, and has no advertising or tracking.

Links and files you share

A share or Continue link holds a copy of your app inside the link itself. Anyone you give the link to can open that copy.
