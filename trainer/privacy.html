Your privacy in the Pattern Trainer

Your photos, webcam pictures and sounds never leave your computer, and they aren't saved anywhere. Closing the tab deletes them.

What happens to your pictures

  • Everything runs inside your browser on your own computer. There's no account, no server doing the work, and no tracking or analytics.
  • When you add a picture, the page turns it into a list of 1280 numbers and keeps those numbers plus a tiny 64×64 preview, only in this tab's memory. The original picture isn't kept.
  • Body poses mode keeps even less: just 33 body points (66 numbers) per example, shown as stick figures. No picture is kept at all.
  • Sounds mode keeps no recordings: each second of sound becomes 521 scores (how much it sounds like speech, music, clapping and so on), shown as a waveform shape. The sound itself is thrown away straight away.
  • Nothing is written to your computer except the files you choose to download.
  • Close or reload the tab and everything is gone, unless you saved a project. The page warns you first.

What's in the file you download

my_model.json, my_pose_model.json or my_audio_model.json holds only your class names, the trained numbers (weights), and a few settings. It contains no pictures or sounds and can't be turned back into them. Open it in any text editor and you'll see it's just numbers. You decide where it goes after you download it.

Project files (my_project.json and so on, from Save project) hold your class names, each example's numbers and the small previews you see on screen. In Pictures mode, those previews are tiny 64×64 copies of your photos, so look after a project file like your photos and don't share it if it shows people. Pose and sound projects only have stick figures and waveform shapes.

The privacy lock

The page carries a browser security rule (a Content Security Policy) that only lets it load its own files from this website. The browser itself blocks it from contacting any other website in the background, so this isn't just a promise.

Check it yourself

  • Wifi test: open the trainer, wait for it to load, then turn off wifi. Recording, training, testing and downloading all still work, because nothing needs the internet.
  • Network test: open your browser's developer tools (F12), choose the Network tab, then record and train. You'll see the page's own files loading at the start and nothing being sent afterwards.

Good habits

Even though nothing is stored, a webcam captures whatever is in front of it. So:

  • Train on things, not people, where you can: objects, drawings, hand signs.
  • If your project needs faces, use only your own. Never use other people's faces or photos without asking them.
  • Check what's in the background before you record.

For teachers: the code is open at github.com/edu-matthew-pham/waypoints-tools (MIT licence). The Python starters run the same way, entirely on the student's computer.
